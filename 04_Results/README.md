# Docking Results

## Binding Affinity

The docking results obtained using AutoDock Vina were compared across the three ligands.

| Ligand | Best Binding Affinity |
|---|---:|
| P83 (PhiKan083) | -5.012 kcal/mol |
| PK7088 | -4.805 kcal/mol |
| Rezatapopt (PC14586) | -4.716 kcal/mol |

## Comparison

Among the three compounds, P83 (PhiKan083) showed the most negative predicted binding affinity under the docking conditions used.

PK7088 and Rezatapopt showed slightly less negative predicted binding affinities.

## Interpretation

The docking scores provide a computational estimate of the relative binding affinity of the compounds toward the TP53 Y220C mutant.

A more negative docking score indicates a more favorable predicted binding score within the scoring framework used by AutoDock Vina.

## Important Note

These docking scores are computational predictions and should not be interpreted as experimentally measured binding affinities or definitive evidence of biological activity. Experimental validation would be required to confirm the predicted interactions.
